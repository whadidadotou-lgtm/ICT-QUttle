Release notes

Update log

1 November 2026

Accounts and settings

  • Added username and password sign-in so players can recover saved profiles after being logged out.
  • Asked existing players to secure their saved progress with a password, and added account editing under Settings.
  • Moved Personalise into the Settings gear menu and kept usernames unique.
  • Made the Settings gear easier to see and added a cinematic lobby soundtrack.
  • Reworked the lobby theme with a distorted electric-guitar riff, power chords, and punchier drums.
  • Expanded progression to level 100 with a new bot unlocked per level and a special unlock sound.
  • Added level-gap PvP limits and boss warnings/music, plus a level-50 parry that reflects fast answers unless a hard question shatters it.
  • Made the original metal soundtrack faster and bigger with layered octave guitars, heavier bass, extra double-kick drums, cymbal crashes, and a more intense low-health mix.
  • Added a triumphant boss-victory fanfare that plays only after winning a boss NPC battle.
  • Added GitHub Pages local mode with browser-saved accounts, progress, practice, and solo bot battles; online play still needs a server.
  • Added separate saved volume sliders for lobby/Pomodoro music and battle/practice sound effects.
29 October 2026

First-person battle effects

  • Added an energy orb that flies to rivals and shrinks on impact, with yellow hard-question shots and larger fast-answer shots.
  • Added first-person health bars, red damage flashes, mini rival health bars, and a larger defeat burst.
29 October 2026

Battle arena refresh

  • Kept players visible in the online roster while they are in a match or accepting an invite.
  • Stopped battle-screen entrance animations from replaying during live state updates.
  • Redesigned the arena with a tactical field, framed fighter panels, and clearer health bars.
  • Added a distinct turn-start chime and final-five-seconds warning cue.
29 October 2026

Gameplay and personalisation

  • Battle questions are now chosen randomly from the selected chapter, regardless of player level.
  • Added subtle fighter and question animations that respect reduced-motion settings.
  • Added optional, locally generated lobby music that pauses in battles and Pomodoro.
  • Improved saved-profile reconnect handling and consolidated a duplicate player save.
28 October 2026

Audio and sound improvements

  • Added a steady, softly mixed drum groove to the generated lo-fi study loop.
  • Added distinct cues for battle starts, hits, misses, victories, defeats, draws, and practice answers.
  • Kept all audio generated locally in the browser, with battle sounds controlled by the Sound toggle.
  • Added this update log so new app changes can be recorded and reviewed in one place.
